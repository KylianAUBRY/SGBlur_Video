# 360° video: equirectangular only, circular padding, wrap-aware linking

## Context and Problem Statement

360° contributions are a key Panoramax use case. Raw camera files come in many
projections, objects cross the 0°/360° seam, and the spherical metadata must
survive re-encoding or players stop showing the video as 360°.

## Decision Drivers

- Correctness at the seam: an object straddling it must be blurred on both sides and keep one identity.
- Scope: v1 must ship; the Panoramax discussion (api#369) leans to equirectangular-only uploads.
- Metadata: FFmpeg reads Spherical V1/V2 but never writes V1 and writes V2 only with `-strict unofficial`.
- Cost: 8K frames; SGBlur's high-resolution pass on 360° images uses the middle band split in two halves.

## Considered Options

1. Equirectangular only; circular padding before detection; post-processing seam stitching; box-level metadata transplant.
2. Reproject to cubemap faces for detection, back-project boxes.
3. Support raw formats (GoPro MAX EAC, Insta360/Theta dual fisheye) in v1.

## Decision Outcome

Chosen option: **1**.

- Detection: spherical side data (or a 2:1 frame without metadata, logged as a heuristic) ⇒ equirectangular. Every pass input gets `EQUIRECT_PAD_RATIO` (1/16 of the width per side) of wrapped pixels; boxes are mapped to canonical coordinates (`x1 ∈ [0, w)`, `x2` may exceed `w`) and duplicates removed with wrap-aware IoU.
- Tiles: SGBlur layout (two halves of the middle band) with circular overlap at both the middle split and the seam.
- Tracking: canonical coordinates; an id break at the seam is repaired in post-processing. *As implemented (see the notes below), this is done by wrap-aware offline linking, not by a separate seam-stitching step: `SEAM_MAX_GAP_S` does not exist.*
- Rendering: shapes crossing `x = w` are blurred on both sides of the seam.
- Metadata: the V1 `uuid` box and V2 `sv3d`/`st3d` boxes are copied from the source by the MP4 box editor (ADR-0008). The local Q360 test files carry V1 only.
- Rejected with `415 unsupported_projection` and a helpful message: GoPro `.360` (two EAC streams), Insta360 `.insv` (dual fisheye; also embeds an unblurred JPEG preview), cubemap/mesh projections.

### Implementation notes (step 7)

- Seam continuity is handled by **wrap-aware offline linking** (ADR-0011) rather than a separate
  stitching pass: distances between fragments are measured around the circle, and a fragment
  joining a chain is shifted by whole turns so interpolation and padding stay continuous.
- At rendering, every shape is blurred together with its copies one turn left and right, so a
  region crossing `x = width` is blurred on both edges.
- A sign annotation crossing the seam keeps its larger visible part (Panoramax shapes cannot wrap).
- FFmpeg only recognises Spherical V1 XMP that contains `StitchingSoftware`, `Spherical`,
  `Stitched` and `ProjectionType`; the original boxes are copied verbatim, never regenerated.
- Measured on the maintainer's 8K video: a synthetic face crossing the seam is one chain and
  blurred on 100 % of its frames (60 leaked object-frames without seam handling); circular
  padding costs ≈ 35 % more analysis time (0.6 s/frame instead of 0.44 s on an M4 Pro).

### Consequences

- Good: simple, matches SGBlur's 360° logic and the likely Panoramax direction; seam handled explicitly and tested with synthetic videos.
- Bad: equirectangular distortion near the poles reduces detection quality there (nadir: car roof, camera holder); `DETECT_PROFILE=thorough` adds tiles over the full height.
- Option 2 is the natural evolution (perspective faces suit the detector better) and is documented as future work, as is native GoPro EAC support.
